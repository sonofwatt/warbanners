Look reference: B, the diorama

This is the direction sketch David picked on 2026-11-18 from three options. It is a rough sketch of the feel, not final art. The scene: a Spartan city in the middle, a hoplite on the left, a Mongol horse archer on the right, mountain and fruit field behind, water and forest in front.

What the sketch fixes

  • Pointy-top hexes seen from a tilt: vertical scale about 0.62 of horizontal.
  • Thick tiles: a visible side wall about 0.3 of the tile radius, in two darker shades of the top colour (left face lighter than right).
  • Features and figures stand up from the tile and overlap the tile behind. Drawn back to front.
  • Figures are chunky and about one tile tall: big head, simple body, one or two readable props (shield and spear, horse and bow).
  • Flat fills, no outlines, no gradients. A soft shadow blob under each figure.
  • Bright, friendly palette: fresh green fields, sky-blue water, warm sand for cities, stone grey mountains with white caps.
  • Faction colour carried by clothing and roofs: Spartans crimson with bronze, Mongols blue with gold.

The options not chosen

  • A, tabletop: flat tiles from above with round unit counters and a health bar.
  • C, campaign map: inked parchment with banners for armies.

David picked B without giving a reason. Claude's reading is that it comes closest to the toy-like feel he said he would miss.
