# Credits

## Sound effects

The recorded sound effects in `public/sounds/` are from Kenney (https://kenney.nl), released under CC0 1.0 Universal
(public domain). CC0 asks for no credit; we give it anyway, with thanks.

Packs used:

- Impact Sounds
- RPG Audio
- Interface Sounds
- Music Jingles

The takes were trimmed, levelled and encoded to mono AAC for the game. The licence text is in
`public/sounds/KENNEY-LICENSE-CC0.txt`. That one licence text (from the Impact Sounds pack) covers all four packs,
which are all CC0.
