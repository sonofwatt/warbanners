# Tech tree rework (rules 8): design and build brief

Decided by David on 2026-11-23 and 24. Ships in rules 8 (0.6.0) on branch `mountains`, after the rules 8 additions
(docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-23-rules8-additions.md). Every number in `CONFIG` or the data tables, never inline. No
em or en dashes anywhere. Originality: our own names and numbers.

## Shape

Each branch has one column 1 tech (a simple resource). It leads to two column 2 techs: the top one unlocks a unit, the
bottom one a tile improvement. Each column 2 tech leads to one column 3 tech: top, a stronger unit; bottom, an upgrade
of the column 2 improvement. A column 3 tech needs only its own column 2 tech.

| Branch | Column 1 | Column 2 top (unit) | Column 3 top | Column 2 bottom (improvement) | Column 3 bottom (upgrade) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Orchards | Foraging: harvest fruit | Defender | Walls | Orchard | Plantation |
| Hunting | Trapping: trap game | Archer | Catapult | Lumber camp | Lumber mill |
| Mountains | Prospecting: gather jewels; climbing mountains (`CONFIG.mountain.tech`) | Spearman, Hoplite (Spartans) | Swordsman | Mine | Foundry |
| Plains | Gathering: harvest wild grain | Horseman, Horse Archer (Mongols), Camp (Mongols), Roads (Spartans) | Knight | Farm | Granary, Market |
| Waters | Fishing: catch fish | (naval units, later) | (later) | (later) | (later) |

Tech ids: our own (for example foraging, defence, walls, orchards, plantations; trapping, archery, siege, logging,
milling; prospecting, spears, smithing, mining, foundry; gathering, riding, chivalry, farming, granary; fishing). Pick
short ids and say them in the report.

## Rules

- **Price:** every tech in a column costs the same: base 4, 6, 8 for columns 1, 2, 3, plus (cities held - 1) x column,
  as today.
- **Start:** each faction starts with one column 1 tech only: Spartans Prospecting, Mongols Gathering. Starting units
  are still placed (Spartan Scout, Mongol Horseman), but a player trains Horsemen, Spearmen and so on only after the
  column 2 tech.
- **Harvests keep the tile:** harvesting fruit (orchard resource) or wild grain (cropland) or trapping game takes the
  one-off growth and marks the resource as used, but the tree, cropland or game tile stays a tile of that resource kind
  for building on. The Orchard is built on a harvested fruit tile (or an unharvested one, which then gives no harvest
  any more), the Farm on cropland, the Lumber camp on forest as today. Model this with a per-tile "harvested" flag
  rather than clearing `resource`.
- **New resources:** jewels (on mountains) and fish (on water). Gathered like today's resources: a one-off growth for
  a gold cost, only on tiles in the player's own territory. Mapgen places them at modest rates, never on a capital's
  tile, mirrored like every other resource. Jewels are gathered from the city (no unit needs to stand on the mountain).
- **Upgrades:** Plantation on an Orchard, Lumber mill on a Lumber camp, Foundry on a Mine, Granary on a Farm. An upgrade
  replaces its improvement on the same tile, costs more and gives more growth than the improvement did (set first
  numbers yourself, for example cost 7 and growth +3, in CONFIG; they get tuned by sims). The Market keeps its rules
  and moves to the Granary tech. Walls keep their rules and move to the Walls tech (Mongols only, as today).
- **Moved unlocks:** Roads with Riding (Spartans only, since Mongols build no roads in rules 8); the Camp with Riding
  (Mongols).
- Provenance: the rules 7 and earlier playouts must still replay, so keep the old tree reachable behind a switch (for
  example `CONFIG.techTree: 'rules7' | 'rules8'`).

## What changes

- Engine: techs table, types, research legality, harvest and building handlers, mapgen, faction starts.
- AI: it researches through the same legality checks; give it sensible priorities for the new tree (its unit techs
  now cost a step more) and check it still trains every unit kind. Keep changes small and report them.
- UI: the tech screen shows five branches, each a column 1 tech splitting into two rows (top and bottom). Locked unit
  and build buttons still open the tech tree at the right tech. New resource and improvement art in the board's
  current style (small SVG marks), tile info text, `text.ts`.
- Rules docs: section 6 (actions table), section 7 (the new tree), the Rules 8 changes list, whatsNew.
- Tests and pins as usual; one heavy suite at a time.
