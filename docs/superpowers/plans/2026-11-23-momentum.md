# Momentum (rules 7, folded in): design and build brief

Decided by David on 2026-11-22 and 2026-11-23. Momentum replaces the rules 5 chain health cost and the rules 7 brace
chain stop. It joins rules 7 on `connected-cities` with hunker and the phalanx brace, before the branch merges; the
rules 7 balance runs are redone with it in. `RULES_VERSION` stays 7 (rules 7 is not released yet).

## The rule

- A unit with the `chain` trait has **momentum**. It starts each of its owner's turns at its start value: Knight 7,
  Khan's Guard 8. A chain unit that appears mid-turn (trained, a reward, a camp) starts with its start value too.
  Units without `chain` have none (0, never shown).
- Every unit type has a **momentum price**. When a chain unit kills a unit with an attack, the kill costs the dead
  unit's price. If the price is no more than the momentum left, it is paid (momentum goes down by the price) and the
  kill gives one more attack this turn, as the chain trait does now. There is no refund after a kill.
- A chain unit may attack any legal target, whatever it costs. If the target's price is more than the momentum left,
  the shortfall cuts the attack: its damage goes down by 1 for each missing point, never below 1. That attack gives no
  extra attack even if it kills, and the momentum left drops to 0. The counter-attack is not changed by the cut.
- The price check applies to every attack a chain unit makes, the first of the turn included. A kill on the first
  attack costs momentum too. (With start values of 7 and 8 and prices at most 4, the first attack is never short.)
- If one attack kills several units (splash), only the target's kill is priced and only it can give the extra attack,
  as today.
- No health cost any more: `chainKillCost` and the `chainCost` event go. The rules 6 level-up that ends a chain stays
  as it is (a hero trait earned from the attack takes back the extra attack); the momentum already paid stays paid.
- The phalanx brace keeps its +2 defence against chain attackers. Its chain stop goes: a braced Hoplite instead has
  its own, higher price. Bracing is judged when the attack is made, before the target can fall.

## Prices (draft, in `CONFIG`, David may change them later)

| Price | Units |
|---|---|
| 0 | Scout |
| 1 | Warrior, Archer, Horseman, Spearman, Catapult, Piper, Sky Caller |
| 2 | Hoplite, Swordsman, Horse Archer, Knight, Sellsword, Camp |
| 3 | Defender, King's Guard, Khan's Guard |
| 4 | a braced Hoplite (in place of its 2) |

Examples: a Knight with 7 kills three Hoplites (2 each, 1 left); a fourth Hoplite is 1 short, so that attack does 1
less damage and ends the chain. Scouts cost nothing: a chain through a line of Scouts is allowed (David: a player who
lines up Scouts should pay for it).

## Numbers

All in `CONFIG`, never inline, for example `momentum: { start: { knight: 7, khansGuard: 8 }, price: { scout: 0, ... },
bracedPrice: 4 }`. The start value is looked up by unit type; a type with `chain` but no start value is a test failure.

## What changes

- **Engine** (`src/engine`): a `momentum: number` field on `Unit` (and wherever units are created and reset each turn;
  the state hash and any save shape follow). Helpers in `combat.ts`, exported through the index for the UI and AI:
  the price of a target for an attacker (`momentumPrice(s, target, attacker)`, which uses `bracedAgainst`) and the
  damage cut (`momentumShort`, the missing points, 0 when paid or when the attacker has no chain). The attack handler
  applies the cut to the damage, pays the price on a kill, grants the extra attack only when paid, and emits events:
  - replace `chainCost` with `{ type: 'momentum', unit, spent, left }` after a paid chain kill (after `died` and
    `advanced`, where `chainCost` was);
  - a short attack that kills emits `{ type: 'chainEnded', unit }` (the existing event and its "Chain ends" text).
  Remove `CONFIG.chainKillCost` and the brace chain stop. Keep `levelUpEndsChain`.
- **Tests:** start values and the per-turn reset; a trained chain unit's start; prices paid on kills; extra attack only
  when paid; the shortfall cut (1 per missing point, minimum 1) and no extra attack on a short kill, with momentum to 0;
  a short attack that does not kill; first-attack kill pays; braced Hoplite costs 4 and still has +2 defence; a Scout
  costs 0 and chains on; Khan's Guard starts with 8; counter unchanged; splash kills unpriced; level-up still ends the
  chain after paying; no health is lost on a chain kill any more. Replace or delete the rules 5 chain-cost tests and the
  brace chain-stop tests, and say which in the report.
- **Combat preview:** whatever the UI shows for expected damage uses the same cut, so a preview against a target the
  unit cannot pay for matches the result.
- **AI** (`src/ai`): its damage estimates use the engine's cut (reuse the engine functions, never a copy), and its chain
  planning counts momentum (no extra attack after a kill it cannot pay for; prefer kills it can pay for when the rest
  is equal). Keep the planner change small. It must not get worse: run the AI tests; re-pin fingerprints and speed
  digests only where the rule change explains it, and say which in the report.
- **UI:** a chain unit's card shows a "Momentum" line, the momentum left of its start value (e.g. "Momentum 5 of 7"),
  text in `src/ui/text.ts`. The `momentum` event shows a short number over the unit in the hero tone (for example
  "-2 momentum", text in `text.ts`), where the chain cost number was. Sound: the `momentum` event plays whatever the
  `chainCost` event played; no new or removed sound cue (David hears every sound change before it ships).
- **Rules docs:** `docs/rules/stage1-rules.md`: the chain trait row, the phalanx row (the brace no longer stops the
  chain; a braced Hoplite costs 4), the rules 7 list (line ~404) and the changelog. `docs/rules/stage2-rules.md`
  section 9 becomes "Momentum (rules 7, replacing the rules 5 chain kill cost)" with the rule, the table and the
  events. `whatsNew` (in `text.ts`, 0.5.0): replace the brace's "gets no extra attack" clause with momentum in one or
  two plain sentences.
- No em or en dashes anywhere (project CLAUDE.md). Unicode minus is fine; a plain hyphen is better.
