# Blockade (rules 8): design and build brief

Decided by David on 2026-11-24. Ships in rules 8 (0.6.0) on branch `mountains`. Every number in `CONFIG`, never
inline; the numbers below are first drafts for the sims to tune. No em or en dashes anywhere.

## The rule

- A city is **blockaded** at the start of its owner's turn (when income is paid) if at least
  `CONFIG.blockade.minTiles` (3, decided by David 2026-11-24, not a tuning lever) of the tiles next to it hold enemy
  units (a tile holds at most one unit, so this counts occupied tiles). Count every enemy unit there, seen or not (the
  engine knows), but never the city's own tile.
- A blockade **suppresses** that city's income, never stops it: the city loses `CONFIG.blockade.cut` (0.5) of its
  income for that turn, rounded down but never less than 1 gold (David), and always keeps at least 1 gold.
- **Walls** soften it: a city with Walls loses `CONFIG.blockade.cutWalls` (0.25) instead, rounded down.
- Whatever the share (no defence, Walls or the Spartan tech), a blockade costs at least `CONFIG.blockade.minCut` (1 gold,
  decided by David 2026-11-24, fixed like the 3 tiles): cut = max(1, floor(income x share)), and the city still keeps at
  least 1 gold, so only a city earning exactly 1 loses nothing.
- **Spartans** still cannot build Walls. Their column 3 tech in the Orchards branch (tech id `walls`, shown to Spartans
  under its own name, our own, for example "Deep stores") gives every Spartan city a better blockade defence:
  `CONFIG.blockade.cutSpartanTech` (0.15), rounded down. It is a faction-wide effect from research, no building.
  Mongols keep Walls in that slot, as today.
- Growth, research, training and healing are not changed by a blockade.
- Provenance: a switch (`CONFIG.blockade.on`, or null numbers) so rules 7 playouts replay unchanged.

## What changes

- Engine: income step in `turn.ts` (or wherever income is paid), a pure helper exported through the index (for
  example `blockadeOf(s, cityId)` returning enemies counted and the gold cut) for the UI and AI; faction or tech data
  for the Spartan effect; an event or field the UI can show.
- AI (small): it should value standing next to an enemy city with spare units when that blockades it, and Spartans
  should buy their column 3 Orchards tech when blockades hurt, Mongols Walls likewise. Keep the change small.
- UI: the city card shows "Blockaded: -N gold" when it is, the tech card for Walls and for the Spartan tech say what
  they do against blockades, and the turn-start income line shows the cut. Text in `text.ts`.
- Docs: `docs/rules/stage1-rules.md` (income section, tech table, Rules 8 changes list), whatsNew one sentence.
- Tests: blockaded at 3 enemy tiles, not at 2; own tile not counted; cut rounding, the 1 gold minimum cut and the 1 gold the city keeps; Walls cut; Spartan
  tech cut; no effect on growth; switch off replays rules 7.
