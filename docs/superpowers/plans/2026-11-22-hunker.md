# Hunker (rules 7, folded in): design and build brief

Decided by David on 2026-11-22. Hunker joins rules 7 on `connected-cities` before that branch merges, and the rules 7
balance runs are redone afterwards with it in.

## The rule

- **Hunker** is a unit action. A unit may hunker only if it has not moved, attacked or used an ability this turn, is
  not fresh (trained this turn), is not already hunkered, and does not have the `siege` trait (the Catapult never
  hunkers).
- Hunkering uses the unit's turn (no movement or attack left), but it is not "doing something" for resting: a unit
  that only hunkered still rests at the start of its owner's next turn.
- A hunkered unit stays hunkered across turns until it moves, attacks, uses an ability, captures, claims a treasure
  or takes any other action of its own. Being attacked, striking back, being healed, taunted or blinded do not end it.
- **Defence:** while hunkered the unit has +1 defence (flat) whenever it is the defender, before terrain cover
  (`def + 1`, then `* cover`). Only when it is attacked; it has no effect when it attacks (which ends it anyway).
- **Resting heal while hunkered:** the regular resting heal + 1 (own territory 5, unclaimed 3, enemy territory 2).
- **Enemy beside it:** resting normally heals nothing while a visible enemy stands next to the unit. A hunkered unit
  still heals then, at half the regular resting heal rounded up, without the +1: own 2, unclaimed 1, enemy 1.
  (Ruling by the controller: "half of the regular amount" read as the regular amount without the bonus, rounded up
  so the enemy-land case is not zero. Cheap to change: one config value.)
- **Ability heal:** a Piper or Sky Caller heal on a hunkered unit gives +1 more (5 instead of 4), up to its maximum.
- Numbers live in `CONFIG` (for example `hunker: { def: 1, restBonus: 1, abilityHealBonus: 1 }`), never inline.

## What changes

- Engine: the action, a `hunkered` flag on units (in state, hashed, visible to both players through `playerView`), the
  defence and heal rules, events (`hunkered`, and the flag clearing), legal actions, refusal reasons. Tests for each
  bullet above, including the Catapult refusal, persistence over turns, each way it ends, the heal numbers and the
  enemy-beside case.
- AI: the planner may hunker. Keep it simple: hunker a wounded unit that has no good attack and cannot reach safety,
  and a unit standing on or beside its own city with an enemy within reach, when it has no good attack. The AI must
  not get worse; run the existing AI tests.
- UI: a Hunker button with the unit card's actions (top right), a "Hunkered" line or badge on the unit card, and a
  small shield mark on the board figure so both players see it. Text in `src/ui/text.ts`. No new sound (David hears
  every sound change first).
- Rules: `docs/rules/stage1-rules.md` section 11 (an ability-like action) and the resting paragraph; a changelog
  line under rules 7. `whatsNew` for 0.5.0 mentions hunker.
- No em or en dashes anywhere (project CLAUDE.md).
